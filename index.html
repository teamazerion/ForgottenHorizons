BEYOND THE FINAL TRANSMISSION

Every mission leaves something behind.

Forgotten Horizons is an independent educational storytelling project about the spacecraft and equipment left behind during exploration of the Moon and Mars. Through mission records, scientific discoveries, and historical moments, we explore what these machines accomplished — and what remains.
