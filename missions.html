THE JOURNEY THROUGH TIME

When the Machines Fell Silent.

Every mission has a beginning, a discovery, and a final transmission. Explore the moments that became part of our history.

1967 — THE MOON

Surveyor 3

Landed on April 20. Apollo 12 astronauts later visited the spacecraft in November 1969.

1969 — THE MOON

Apollo Lunar Module

Apollo 11 landed on July 20, leaving its lunar module descent stage at the historic landing site.

1976 — MARS

Viking 1 & Viking 2

Two pioneering landers reached Mars and began investigating its soil, atmosphere, and environment.

1997 — MARS

Mars Pathfinder & Sojourner

Pathfinder landed on July 4. Sojourner explored nearby rocks and soil, operating for 83 days.

2004 — MARS

Spirit & Opportunity

Two rovers began exploring Mars, searching for evidence of ancient environments shaped by water.

2010 — SPIRIT

A Rover Falls Silent

Spirit's last communication was received on March 22, 2010, after years of exploration.

2018 — OPPORTUNITY

The Final Signal

Opportunity last communicated on June 10, 2018, after nearly 15 years exploring Mars.
